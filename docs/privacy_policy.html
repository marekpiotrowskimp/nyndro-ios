Privacy Policy for Nyndro

Last updated: December 2024

Nyndro ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our Buddhist practice tracking application handles your information.

Data Collection

Nyndro does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers.

All your practice data remains on your device. We do not have access to your practice records, history, or any personal information.

Data Storage

The following data is stored locally on your device:

  • Practice Records: Your practice entries, repetition counts, and history are stored using Apple's SwiftData framework
  • User Preferences: Settings such as sound preferences, haptic feedback, and display options are stored using UserDefaults
  • Reminders: Any practice reminders you set are stored locally and managed through iOS notifications

Data Sharing

We do not share any data with third parties. Your practice data never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to export it.

Data Export

Nyndro provides the ability to export your data in JSON or CSV format. This feature is entirely user-initiated and the exported data is handled directly by you through iOS sharing mechanisms.

Analytics & Tracking

Nyndro does not use any analytics services, tracking tools, or advertising SDKs. We do not track your usage patterns or collect any metrics.

Notifications

If you enable practice reminders, the app uses iOS local notifications. These notifications are processed entirely on your device and no notification data is sent to external servers.

Children's Privacy

Nyndro does not knowingly collect any information from children. The app is suitable for users of all ages as it does not collect personal information.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

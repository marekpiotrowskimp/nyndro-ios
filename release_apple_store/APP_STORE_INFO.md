## 2. Description

### English (max 4000 characters)

```
Nyndro is a mindful practice tracking app designed for Buddhist practitioners following the Ngöndro path.

TRACK YOUR PROGRESS
• Count your daily practice repetitions with a simple tap
• Track progress toward traditional goals (100,000+ repetitions)
• View beautiful lotus or mala-style progress visualizations

SMART PREDICTIONS
• Get insights on when you'll complete your practice goals
• See daily, weekly, and monthly statistics
• Track your practice streaks and patterns

MINDFUL FEATURES
• Set gentle reminders to maintain daily practice
• Keep screen awake during sessions
• Choose from calming sounds: bell, singing bowl, gong
• Haptic feedback for each count

EXPORT & BACKUP
• Export your data as JSON or CSV
• Import data to transfer between devices
• Never lose your practice progress

Built with mindfulness for the benefit of all sentient beings.
```

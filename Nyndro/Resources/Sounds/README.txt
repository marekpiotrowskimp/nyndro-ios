Sound Files Required for Nyndro iOS
====================================

Please add the following audio files to this folder:

1. click.wav (or .mp3)
   - Short, subtle click sound
   - Used for: counter taps, basic feedback

2. bell.wav (or .mp3)
   - Gentle bell/chime sound
   - Used for: medium milestone celebrations

3. singing_bowl.wav (or .mp3)
   - Tibetan singing bowl sound
   - Used for: meditation feedback

4. gong.wav (or .mp3)
   - Deep gong or bowl sound
   - Used for: major milestones, practice completion

Recommended sources:
- freesound.org (free, royalty-free sounds)
- zapsplat.com (free with attribution)
- Record your own with a real singing bowl

Format requirements:
- WAV or MP3 format
- Sample rate: 44100 Hz
- Duration: 0.5-3 seconds
